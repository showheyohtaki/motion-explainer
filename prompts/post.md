# Prompt: the post text

The post carries four attachments: video 1, video 2, video 3 and the cheat sheet, all 1200x1350, in that order (on X they show as a 2x2 grid: top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right).

The text is short on purpose. The videos do the explaining. The original posts used this flow:

1. **What it is:** "a cheat sheet of the names of <topic> for asking AI"
2. **The problem:** the vague request people make today, and that it rarely gets the result
3. **The fix:** three of the names in quotes, and that asking by name gets you much closer
4. **Save it:** one short line

## Examples (the original posts)

English:

```
A cheat sheet of 24 camera moves & transitions for your AI video prompts 🎬

"Make it look cinematic" rarely gets you the shot you want.

Ask by name — "FPV," "rack focus," "whip pan" — and you'll get much closer to what you have in mind.

Bookmark this 🔖
```

Japanese:

```
AIへの指示用に、"モーショングラフィックスの演出の名前" をまとめました！

たとえばClaudeやCodexで動く図や動画を作るとき、「気持ちよく動かして」だけでは、狙った動きはまず出ない。

「スタガー」「オーバーシュート」「マスクトランジション」と名前で頼めば、思い通りの動きを再現できます。

保存推奨です🔖
```

## The request

```
Write the post text for this set in the set's language (the language the user speaks to you in), following the 4-part flow and the examples
in prompts/post.md.
- Topic: <TOPIC>. Audience: <AUDIENCE>. The tools they use: <e.g. AI image models>.
- The vague request should be something this audience really types or says.
- Pick three names from the list that the audience half-knows: recognizable, but not
  something they already use every day. Use them exactly as they are in the video.
- No numbers, results or promises you cannot back up. "Much closer" is fine; "perfect
  every time" is not.
- Plain wording. At most one exclamation mark and one or two emoji.
- Keep it short enough that the last line shows without "Show more" (about 280 characters
  on X; count a CJK character as two).
- End with the hashtag #MotionExplainer on its own line.
Give 2 versions.
```

## Tips

- Add **#MotionExplainer** at the end. Other people who make these sets look for it (the original posts above came before the tag).
- Use the same names in the post as in the videos and on the sheet. Readers compare.
- If the set is in a language most of your followers do not read, post it on its own rather than as a reply to the original.
- If you made earlier sets, link them in replies to the new post, a little while after posting.

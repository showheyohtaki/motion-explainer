The list behind the videos in `en/` and `ja/`. Same order as the cards and the cheat sheet.
For people who make videos with AI video models or code-based video tools.

Bottom line of the cheat sheet:
- EN: Add timing, speed, and direction for better results: "over 3 seconds" / "slowly" / "toward the subject"
- JA: 名前に『秒数・速さ・被写体』を添えると精度が上がる：3秒かけて ／ ゆっくり ／ 人物に向かって

## Notes from making it

- Look-alike pairs were put side by side and built to differ: dolly in and zoom in end with the figure at the same size, but in the dolly the background barely grows (the ratio between near and far changes) while in the zoom everything grows together. A small focal-length readout (24mm stays / 24→87mm) helps.
- The dolly zoom keeps the figure the same size (dotted guides at head and feet) while only the background changes.
- J-cut and L-cut cannot be shown with picture alone, so those cards add two small bars (video and audio) to show the audio leading or trailing.
- The English version was checked by a separate reviewer for natural English. That review changed the title, the subtitle, the bottom line and five descriptions (for example "Raise the camera upward" → "Lift the camera up").
- The REC dot blinks a whole number of times in 9.4 seconds, so the loop has no seam.

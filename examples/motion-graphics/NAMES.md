# Motion graphics: the 24 names

The list behind the videos in `ja/` (Japanese labels with English names). Same order as the cards and the cheat sheet.
For people who make motion graphics or animated diagrams, by hand or with AI.

| | |
|---|---|
| Look | Each card is a small shot from a motion-design reel: a navy stage with one palette of four colors (blue, coral, yellow, mint) used on real-looking subjects (an app icon, a bar chart, a headline, a badge). The page frame stays gray and blue |
| Japanese title | モーショングラフィックス チートシート 24 |
| Why color here | When the subject of a card is the movement itself, color makes the movement easier to follow. In the camera-moves set the opposite was true: color on the background hid what the card was about |

The "English" column below is a translation for readers of this file. Only the Japanese description is on the cheat sheet.

## Notes from making it

- This is the second version. The first one spent a whole video on eight easing curves (linear, ease-in, ease-out ...) with dots moving the same distance. It was correct but plain, and a speed difference is hard to see. The remake keeps one "Easing" card that runs a constant-speed lane and an eased lane side by side, and fills the rest with moves that have a visible result.
- Each card was treated as a cut from a motion-design reel, not a diagram: a real-looking subject (icon, chart, headline, badge) with the move applied to it.
- Look-alike moves were given different subjects so they can be told apart: pop-in (an icon) vs overshoot (a meter needle), burst (lines from a heart) vs particles (confetti), and the three transitions 17-19 on the same day/night screen with only the transition changed.
- The 4.7-second cycles of the eight cards start at different times so their key moments do not happen at once.
- Glitch, light sweep and impact shake were measured for flashing: no light/dark swing three or more times a second.
- The cheat-sheet icons are small color thumbnails of each card, drawn by an image model from a contact sheet of the video (see `prompts/cheatsheet-image.md`, option B).
